# Play on another device, and the phone as a remote (M6)

Every device with Hive open (signed in as the same person) knows about the
others through home.

- **Move playback:** Now playing → the devices button (next to Like) →
  **Play on** lists *This device* and your other devices with Hive open,
  with what each is playing. Pick one: the same queue carries on there from
  the same moment, and it pauses here.
- **Be the remote:** this device then shows **Playing on Bilal's
  MacBook** above the tabs: play/pause and next right there; tap it for the
  full remote with the song, scrubbing, previous/next and the other
  device's volume.
- **Bring it back:** on the remote, **Play here instead** picks up the song
  (and its queue) here from where it is and pauses the other device. **Stop
  being the remote** just stops controlling it.
- Picking a device while nothing plays here makes this one its remote
  without moving anything.

Only the person's own devices see and control each other, and only while
Hive is open on them (a device that closes Hive drops out of the list).
