# Music videos (M6)

Put video files in the music folders and Hive plays each with its song.

**Naming** (either works):

- Beside the song, with the same name: `Premam/03 Malare.mp4` next to
  `Premam/03 Malare.flac`.
- Anywhere in the music folders, as `Artist - Title.mp4`:
  `Videos/Vineeth Sreenivasan - Aluva Puzha (Official Video).mp4`. Words in
  brackets and a leading track number are ignored.

MP4, M4V, MOV, MKV and WebM are found; MP4 (H.264/AAC) plays everywhere,
the others depend on the device. Hive streams the file as it is.

**In the apps:** when the playing song has a video, Now playing shows a
clapperboard. The video starts from where the song is; **Back to the song**
carries the song on from where the video got to.

**Hive Admin → Library → Music videos** shows how many matched and lists the
ones that didn't, so they can be renamed. Videos are matched after each
library scan (or Library → Scan now).
