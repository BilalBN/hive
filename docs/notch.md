# Song controls by the Mac's notch (M6)

On a Mac with a notch (MacBook Pro 2021 and later, MacBook Air M2 and
later), point at the notch while Hive plays: a black panel grows out of it
with the cover, title and artist, previous, play/pause and next. It also
shows for a moment when the song changes. It works while you're in another
app and on every desktop, including full-screen apps.

Settings → On this computer → **Song controls by the notch** turns it off.
On a Mac without a notch nothing shows. The iPhone already has the same
through the Dynamic Island and lock screen.
